# Using Nucleus from Slack

Mention the bot in an approved channel with your request:

> @nucleus Our softgel dissolution is slowing at the 6-month accelerated
> timepoint. The fill is PEG 400 with polysorbate 80. What is happening?

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement** — a run reference, immediately.
2. **Plan** — which specialists will be consulted and why. Broad, costly or
   regulated requests pause here for your approval.
3. **Progress** — each specialist as it completes.
4. **Executive Decision Brief** — conclusion, recommendation, confidence, risks,
   unresolved disagreements, assumptions, and any decisions needed from you.

## Approvals

Nucleus stops and asks when a request touches a regulated decision — batch
release, a submission, shelf-life assignment, compliance sign-off — or when the
recommendation itself is AMBER or RED. It will prepare and recommend; it never
executes those decisions.

## What it will not do

- Assert a fact it cannot evidence. Unevidenced claims are labelled assumptions.
- Invent study results, analytical values, stability data or regulatory positions.
- Present its output as a regulatory approval, GMP release or validated state.
- Hide a specialist failure behind a clean-looking answer.

If the data it needs does not exist, it will tell you what to go and get.
